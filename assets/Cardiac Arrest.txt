Non-shockable
Non-shockable Rhythm
1 Communicate with team
Designate a code leader
2 Check airway and IV access
3 Start compressions 100 – 120 bpm, 
Compress 1/3 – ½ of chest diameter
Do not lean on chest, allow recoil
Consider open chest/direct cardiac compression, esp. during surgery of a body cavity
Continue uninterrupted for 2 minutes
4 Discontinue inhalant delivery
Flush breathing circuit with O2
5 Ventilate 8-12 breaths per minute, 10 mL/kg
6 Administer [Drugs (Epinephrine) 70] 0.01 mg/kg IV 
Alternative: [Drugs (Vasopressin) 260] 0.8 U/kg IV 
7 Administer atropine 
Administer [Drugs (Atropine) 475] 0.04 mg/kg IV once 
8 Reverse opioids and benzodiazepines
See [Drugs (Naloxone) 1924] and [Drugs (Flumazenil) 1924]
Consider reversing alpha2 agonists IV [Drugs (Atipamazole) 1304]
9 If ETCO2 <18 mmHg, re-assess technique
10 Change compressor personnel every 2 minutes
Assess ECG during switch
Consider transthoracic ultrasound of cardiac contractility & filling
11 Repeat [Drugs (Epinephrine) 80] 0.01 mg/kg IV q 3 – 5 minutes 
12 If CPR >15 min 
Consider [Drugs (Sodium Bicarb) 2150]

Shockable
Shockable Rhythm
1 Communicate with team 
Designate a code leader
Charge defibrillator
2 Check airway and IV access
3 Start compressions 100 – 120 bpm, 
Compress 1/3 – 1/2 of chest diameter
Do not lean on chest, allow recoil
Consider open chest/direct cardiac compression, esp. during surgery of a body cavity
Continue uninterrupted for 2 minutes
4 Discontinue inhalant delivery
Flush breathing circuit with O2
5 Ventilate 8-12 breaths per minute, 10 mL/kg
6 Reverse opioids and benzodiazepines
See [Drugs (Naloxone) 1924] and [Drugs (Flumazenil) 1924]
Consider reversing alpha2 agonists IV [Drugs (Atipamazole) 1304]
7 Pause and defibrillate
Call CLEAR
First shock 
  External: 2 J/kg [Drugs (Shock) 634]
  Internal: 0.2  J/kg [Drugs (Shock) 634]
8 2 minutes of uninterrupted chest compressions
Call CLEAR
Subsequent shocks
  External: 4 J/kg [Drugs (Shock) 634]
  Internal: 0.4 J/kg [Drugs (Shock) 634]
9 If rhythm does not convert after 2 shocks,
Administer [Drugs (Epinephrine) 65] 0.01 mg/kg or [Drugs (Vasopressin) 240] 0.8 U/kg q 3-5 min 
Consider [Drugs (Esmolol) 1500] 0.5 mg/kg IV 3–5 min, then infusion at 50 µg/kg/min 
Dogs: Administer [Drugs (Lidocaine) 2100] 2 mg/kg IV 
Cats : Administer [Drugs (Amiodarone) 1112] 5 mg/kg IV
