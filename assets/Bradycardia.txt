Dx
Dog | HR <60 bpm with hypotension | or | HR <30 bpm || Cat | HR <100 bpm with hypotension | or | HR <80 bpm

Tx
1 Communicate with team
Stop surgical manipulation
Call for help
2 Check the patient:
Pulse quality, blood pressure
If no pulse/HR, start CPR [Cardiac Arrest]
Confirm adequate oxygenation/ventilation. If hypoxemia go to [Hypoxemia]
3 Atropine 
Administer [Drugs (Atropine) 320] 0.04 mg/kg IV 
Consider repeating [Drugs (Atropine) 320]
4 Decrease or stop all anesthetics 
Consider reversal of alpha-2 agonists [Drugs (Atipamezole) 10] 
Consider reversal of opioids [Drugs (Naloxone) 909]
5 If atropine ineffective:
[Drugs (Epinephrine) 535] bolus 0.5 - 1 mcg / kg
Infusion of [Drugs (Epinephrine) 1000] 
Infusion of [Drugs (Dobutamine) 2000]
Consider [Hyperkalemia]
Cardiology consult +/- pacing
