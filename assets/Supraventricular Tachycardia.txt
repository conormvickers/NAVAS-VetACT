Dx
Supraventricular Tachycardia | [assets/svt.jpeg] | Narrow complex tachycardia
Differentiate from SINUS tachycardia | more likely SVT if >250 bpm | or | sudden onset/offset

Tx
1 Communicate with team
Call for help
2  Check the patient
Pulse quality/blood pressure 
3  Decrease or stop all anesthetics
4  Attempt vagal maneuver (press on eyes)
5  Rate control drugs:
[Drugs (Diltiazem) 50] slowly IV
[Drugs (Esmolol) 190] slowly IV
Dogs only: Consider [Drugs (Lidocaine) 190]
6 Cardiology consult +/- cardioversion

Drug doses:
Diltiazem IV 0.05 mg/kg (Repeat up to 5 times over 10 minutes) | 0.05 mg/kg
Esmolol IV 0.25 mg/kg (administered in increments over 2 – 5 minutes) | 10 mg/mL
Lidocaine Dogs IV 2 mg/kg | 20 mg/mL
