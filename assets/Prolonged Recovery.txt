Dx
| Most patients regain consciousness within 15-20 minutes 
Exceptions | Unstable patients | Invasive procedures | Total IV anesthesia protocols

Tx
1 Communicate with team
Check the patient’s vital signs
Is airway adequately protected?
Is there hypothermia, hypotension, hypoxemia, or hypercarbia?
2 Ensure all inhalant and injectable anesthetics are discontinued
3 Stimulate the patient (rub vigorously, shift position, make noise)
4 Consider reversal agents
Benzodiazepines: [Drugs (Flumazenil) 100]
Alpha-2 agonists: [Drugs (Atipamazole) 50]
Opioids: consider slow titration of [Drugs (Naloxone) 100]
5 Review anesthetic record
Profound physiologic disturbance?
Relative or absolute drug overdose?
6 Check for hypoglycemia, anemia, electrolyte or acid-base abnormality
7 Consider co-existing disease 
Severe liver disease
Addison’s
Intracranial disease
New neurologic disease (ischemia/increased ICP)

Drug doses:
Atipamazole IM 100 mcg/kg | 5000 mcg/mL
Flumazenil IV 0.01 mg/kg (consider repeating twice) | 0.1 mg/mL
Naloxone IV 0.005 mg/kg (consider repeating up to 0.02 mg/kg) | 0.4 mg/mL
