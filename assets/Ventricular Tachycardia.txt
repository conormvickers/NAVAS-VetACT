Dx
Wide complex tachycardia | HR >180-200 bpm | [assets/hyperkalemia ecg.jpeg] 

Tx
1 Communicate with team
Call for help
2 Check the patient:
Pulse quality, blood pressure
If no pulse/HR, start CPR [Cardiac Arrest]
3 Decrease or stop all anesthetics
4 Administer lidocaine
Cat 0.5 mg/kg IV [Drugs (Lidocaine - cat) 530]
Dog 2 mg/kg IV [Drugs (Lidocaine - dog) 742]
Repeat up to 4 times
5 If lidocaine ineffective
Consider: [Drugs (Amiodarone) 50] or [Drugs (Procainamide) 917] 
Consider: [Drugs (Magnesium Sulfate) 917] or [Drugs (Esmolol) 258] 
6 Consider cardiology consult +/- cardioversion

Drug doses:
Amiodarone IV 5 mg/kg | 1.5 mg/mL
Esmolol IV 0.25 mg/kg (administered in increments over 2 – 5 minutes followed by 10 –200 mcg/kg/min) | 10 mg/mL
Lidocaine Cats IV 0.5 mg/kg | 20 mg/mL
Lidocaine Dogs IV 2 mg/kg | 20 mg/mL
Magnesium Sulfate 30 mg/kg (diluted & admin over 10 minutes, then 15 – 20 mg/kg/hr) | self dilute
Procainamide 2 mg/kg (slowly over 2 minutes, then 10 – 40 mcg/kg/min) | 100 mg/mL
