Dx
|Low arterial oxygen concentration || or || |SpO2 < 94%

Tx
1 Communicate with team
2 Check patient
3 Assess equipment
Confirm flowmeter on (check FiO2 if possible)
Check connections of anesthesia machine, circuit connected and ETT
Assess endotracheal tube depth (not too deep)
4 Ventilate by hand
Assess compliance (ease of ventilation by hand)
Assess presence and shape of capnograph
Listen to lung sounds on both sides of chest
5 Consider:
Suctioning airway
Discontinuing anesthesia
T-FAST/ radiographs
Recruitment maneuver
Bronchodilator [Drugs (Drugs)]
Thoracocentesis 
Arterial blood gas
Repositioning ETT

Drug doses:
Albuterol (1 puff cat/small dog, 2 puffs large dog) | 90 mcg/puff 
Terbutaline SQ 0.01 mg/kg | 1 mg/mL

Causes
Think: Equipment or Patient?
Equipment | O2 supply failure | Circuit mis/disconnected | Airway obstruction | Endobronchial intubation | Extubation
Patient | Respiratory: Apnea, Anaphylaxis, Aspiration, Bronchospasm, Diaphragmatic hernia, Hypoventilation, Obesity, Pleural fluid, Pneumothorax, Pneumonia, Pulmonary edema | Cardiovascular: shunt, CHF, PTE, Low cardiac output | Hemoglobin: Low SpO2, Normal PaO2
