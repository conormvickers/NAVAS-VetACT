Dx
Mean Arterial Pressure | <60 mmHg
Doppler Pressure | <90 mmHg

Tx
1 Communicate with team
Hemorrhage? 
Vascular compression (ex. surgical retraction, insufflation, pneumothorax)?
Anaphylaxis? 
2 Check the patient 
Assess anesthetic depth (see # 4)
Check HR & rhythm, pulse quality, mucous membranes
If bradycardia, give [Drugs (glycopyrrolate) 250 ] infusion 0.01 mg/kg or [Drugs (atropine) 60] infusion 0.02 mg/kg IV, or go to [Bradycardia]
If no pulse/hr go to cardiac arrest flow sheet [Cardiac Arrest]
3 Consider equipment error if patient assessment OK
Retake BP with new machine/ cuff
4 Decrease anesthetic depth (if possible)
Consider additional analgesics
5 Administer IV fluid bolus
Balanced [Drugs (isotonic crystalloids) 450] 5 mL/kg IV over 5 min 
Repeat as necessary
Consider dynamic assessment of fluid responsiveness (e.g., cardiac or vena cava POCUS, PVI/PPV)
Assess total volume delivered (mL/kg)
6 Consider: 
[Drugs (Hypertonic Saline) 450], 
Blood products, 
[Drugs (Artificial colloids) 450]
