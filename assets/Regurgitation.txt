Dx
Regurgitation | Passive movement of stomach contents into mouth/nasopharynx 
Possible Sequelae | Aspiration pneumonia  | Esophagitis/esophageal stricture | Rhinitis/nasopharyngeal stenosis

Tx
1 Communicate with team
Check endotracheal tube seal
Minimum no leak volume 20 cmH2O
Can assess with cuff manometer
2 Flush esophagus with warm tap water
Red rubber catheter, Salem sump, or similar
Catheter tip 35 - 60 mL syringe
Source of suction
Flush until suctioned water is clear
3 If regurgitation noted from nose, flush & suction nasal cavity with saline
4 Consider:
[Drugs (Famotidine) 287] or [Drugs (Pantoprazole) 287] 
Localized [Drugs (Sodium Bicarbonate Infusion) 76]
Oral [Drugs (Sucralfate) 290] once awake 
5 Check oropharynx for fluid/material prior to extubation
6 Monitor for cough, increased respiratory rate, & regurgitation

Drug doses:
Sodium Bicarbonate 10 mL/application (topical application into esophagus, Consider repeating) | 4.2 %solution 
Famotidine 0.5 mg/kg IV  | 10 mg/mL
Pantoprazole 1 mg/kg IV slowly | 4 mg/mL
Sucralfate 1 gm PO q8h (dissolve in wáter to form slurry) | 1 gm tablet
